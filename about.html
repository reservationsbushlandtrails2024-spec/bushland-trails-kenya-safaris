Bushland Trails Kenya Safaris was created with a passion for sharing the authentic beauty, wildlife, culture and people of Kenya.

WHO WE ARE

A growing Kenyan tour operator with a local perspective.

We are a startup tour company founded by experienced tourism professionals with more than seven years of industry experience. Our goal is simple: make it easier for travelers to experience Kenya in a way that feels authentic, seamless and meaningful.

We arrange safaris, day trips, airport and SGR transfers, destination experiences, customized itineraries, flight bookings and visa assistance.
